【主角死因原文（failure_analysis，保真锚点，禁杜撰）】
"Magic Ears died from a lethal combination of regulatory annihilation and unsustainable unit economics, with the former delivering the final blow to an already struggling business model. The immediate cause was China's 'Double Reduction' policy announced in July 2021, which banned for-profit tutoring companies from teaching core curriculum subjects (including English) to K-12 students. This wasn't a gradual regulatory tightening—it was an overnight market elimination. Companies were prohibited from raising capital, going public, or operating on weekends and holidays. ... For Magic Ears, this meant losing access to 100% of its core customer base with no transition period. ... By mid-2021, Magic Ears was likely months away from a down-round or shutdown even without regulatory intervention. The policy change simply made the death instantaneous rather than gradual."

【主角教训原文（startup_learnings 提炼）】
- "Regulatory risk in education markets is existential, not incremental."
- "Never build a company where a single government can eliminate your entire TAM overnight."
- "Unit economics must work at current scale, not theoretical future scale."
- "can AI eliminate or augment the supply side? Modern AI models can now deliver personalized tutoring at near-zero marginal cost."

The wedge is India's 125M English learners who can't afford $50/month platforms but will pay $5/month for AI tutoring."
